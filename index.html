Universal Support

Hardware & Device Compatibility

TapCard Pro works universally with any NFC hardware and modern smartphones.

Apple iPhone Compatibility

  • Native Background Reading (No app needed): iPhone XS, XR, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, and all Pro / Pro Max models.
  • Just tap the top-front or top-back of the iPhone against your card.
  • Older iPhones (iPhone 7, 8, X) can scan via the built-in Control Center NFC reader or camera QR code.

Android Compatibility

  • All Modern Android Smartphones: Samsung Galaxy S & Note series, Google Pixel (all generations), OnePlus, Xiaomi, Sony Xperia, Motorola, and more.
  • Ensure NFC is turned ON in Android Quick Settings.
  • Tap the center back of the phone against the card.
