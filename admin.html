📱 Program a Physical NFC Card

  1. Download NFC Tools (iOS / Android) — free.
  2. Tap Write → Add a Record → Custom URL.
  3. Paste your QR permalink URL and tap Write.
  4. Hold your NFC card/badge to the back of your phone — done!
